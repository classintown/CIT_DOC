
# OV-001 — Welcome to ClassInTown (Instructor-Focused Overview)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Video ID** | OV-001-INSTRUCTOR (variant of OV-001) |
| **Audience** | **Instructors only** — dance teachers, tutors, coaches, music teachers |
| **Length** | ~4–5 min (270 sec) |
| **Route** | `/` → optional `/marketplace` (your listing) |
| **Auth** | None (public homepage) |
| **Goal** | Instructor understands why ClassInTown exists for them, what they get, how students find them, and what to do next. |

## Recording notes

- Keep **"I want to teach"** selected the entire time — never switch to student mode
- Speak to **one instructor**: "you" and "your classes", not "users"
- **Spend 50% of the video** on hero mockup + instructor story sections
- Student/search sections = reframe as **"how parents discover you"**
- End CTA: **List for free** only
- Record Acts 1–5 as separate takes if needed
